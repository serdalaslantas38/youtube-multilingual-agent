OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio

OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio is a creator-operated localization, subtitle validation, and publishing workflow platform built by OrchidHacks (HayataDairPüfNoktalar / @orchidhacks) to manage multilingual video titles, descriptions, and closed captions across 48 languages.

Key Capabilities

  • Channel & Video Synchronization: Connects to the authorized creator's channel to list uploaded videos and inspect source Turkish subtitle tracks.
  • Deterministic Subtitle & Metadata Validation: Translates and validates titles, descriptions, and SRT closed-caption tracks across 48 target languages with human review and content-hash approval gates.
  • Verified Publishing: Publishes approved localized metadata and subtitle tracks back to the creator's own videos with post-write verification and audit logging.

Privacy & Data Protection

OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio accesses Google and YouTube APIs strictly on behalf of the authenticated channel owner. OAuth 2.0 tokens are encrypted at rest with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) and are never shared with third parties. Read our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
