Privacy Policy — OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio

Effective Date: November 2026 · Application: OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio

This Privacy Policy describes how OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, accesses, uses, stores, retains, and protects information obtained from Google and YouTube APIs when an authorized user signs in and connects a YouTube channel.

1. Compliance with Google & YouTube API Policies

OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio uses YouTube API Services. Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. By using this Application, users also acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Access

When you authorize OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio via Google OAuth 2.0, we request and collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the localization workflow:

  • Account Identity Data (Scopes openid, email, profile): Your Google Account email address, display name, and unique subject identifier used to authenticate your operator session and enforce workspace access controls.
  • YouTube Channel & Video Data (Scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl): Your YouTube channel ID, channel title, custom handle, uploads playlist ID, video IDs, source titles, source descriptions, existing video localizations, and closed-caption tracks (captions.list, captions.download, captions.insert, videos.list, videos.update).
  • OAuth Credentials: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens issued by Google to perform authorized API requests on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following user-facing features requested by the authenticated channel owner:

  • Discovering uploaded videos and inspecting source subtitle tracks on the authenticated YouTube channel.
  • Generating, validating, and reviewing translated video titles, descriptions, and SRT subtitle files across configured target languages.
  • Publishing approved localized titles, descriptions, and closed-caption tracks back to the user's own YouTube videos and verifying publication status.
  • Maintaining an internal audit log and daily YouTube API quota ledger for the channel operator.

4. Data Sharing, Third Parties & Limited Use

  • No Sale or Advertising Use: We never sell, rent, license, or transfer Google user data to any third party, data broker, or advertising network.
  • Translation Processing: Only public/creator video text (source titles, descriptions, and subtitle cue text) is processed through configured translation providers solely to produce the user's requested translations. OAuth tokens, email addresses, and account credentials are never sent to translation providers or language models.
  • No Human Access Without Consent: No human other than the authenticated workspace owner/operator may view stored channel data unless explicitly authorized for support or required by law.

5. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

  • All OAuth 2.0 refresh tokens are encrypted at rest before database storage using symmetric authenticated encryption (Fernet: AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256) keyed via a secret environment key.
  • All network communication with Google and YouTube endpoints occurs exclusively over TLS/HTTPS.
  • OAuth tokens and client secrets are never returned in API responses or exposed to browser JavaScript.

6. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

  • Retention: Cached video metadata, localized drafts, and encrypted OAuth tokens are retained only while your channel connection remains active.
  • Revoking Access: You may revoke OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time through the Google Account Permissions page.
  • Deletion Requests: When you disconnect your channel or request deletion, your encrypted OAuth tokens and cached channel records are permanently deleted within 24 hours.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact the OrchidHacks Multilingual Studio administrator via the support email listed on our Google OAuth consent screen or through our project repository at github.com/serdalaslantas38/youtube-multilingual-agent.
